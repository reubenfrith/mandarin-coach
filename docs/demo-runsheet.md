# Mandarin Coach — demo runsheet

Exact inputs to paste / say during the demo recording, in order. Companion to the
Demo section of [`presentation-script.md`](presentation-script.md). The slide is just
the video; narrate over it — except beat C, where you let the recording's audio play.

## Before you record

- **Log in with an account that already has some error history**, so beat B returns a
  real count. Fresh account → the count will be small/zero; if so, run beat A a couple
  of times first, or use the **"New chat"** button (top bar, Text-coach tab) to reset
  the conversation while keeping your logged history (the corpus is per-login, not
  per-conversation).
- **Sound on** for beat C — it's the one clip whose audio should play.
- Turn the **拼音 Show pīnyīn** toggle on so 汉字 + pīnyīn show together.

---

## Beat A — Text coach: a simple correction  *(narrate over)*

**Paste into the chat:**

```
我有两个猫
```

**Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge māo** — "I have two cats."

- **Expect:** flags the **measure word** — animals take **只 (zhī)**, not **个 (ge)** →
  corrects to **我有两只猫 (Wǒ yǒu liǎng zhī māo)**, explains why, shows the **📝 logged** step.
- **Say:** "A simple slip — wrong measure word. It fixes it, tells me *why*, and quietly
  files the mistake."

---

## Beat B — The memory payoff  *(narrate — this is the moment)*

**Type into the chat:**

```
How many measure-word mistakes have I made — am I improving?
```

- **Expect:** an **exact count + trend** over your corpus ("you've made this 6 times…
  trending down"), computed, not estimated.
- **Say:** "*This* is what nothing else does — it remembers, counts, and tells me exactly
  what to drill." Pause here and let it land.

---

## Beat C — Voice: conversation → coaching flip  *(let the recording's audio play)*

**Say out loud in Mandarin:**

> 我想喝咖啡。
> **Wǒ xiǎng hē kāfēi.** — "I want to drink coffee."

- It replies **in spoken Mandarin** (conversation partner).

**Then say in English:**

> "How do I ask for that more politely?"

- **Expect:** the intent router **flips to English coaching** — it breaks the phrase down
  instead of chatting back.
- **Say (after it finishes):** "Notice it switched from partner to coach the second I
  asked a learning question."

Keep this beat to **~15 seconds** so the pause in your narration is short.

---

## Beat D — Pronounce: tone scoring  *(narrate over)*

**Type a draft:**

```
我想学中文
```

**Wǒ xiǎng xué Zhōngwén** — "I want to learn Chinese." → Pass 1 turns it into a clean
target with tone marks.

- **Say it into the mic** → Pass 2 shows the **per-syllable tone verdict + pitch-curve overlay**.
- **Optional kicker:** deliberately say one syllable on the wrong tone (flatten
  **想 xiǎng**) → it flags that syllable **red**.
- **Say:** "Not AI guessing — pure pitch analysis. Speech-to-text hears what I *meant*;
  this hears what I actually *said*."

---

## Notes

- **Core, if you're tight on time: A → B.** That's the differentiator.
- Burn the on-screen 汉字 / pīnyīn into the recording so the point lands even if room
  audio is weak.
- Two audio sources at once (you + the video) muddies it — only one of you talks at a
  time. Talk over A, B, D; stay quiet and let the audio play for C.
- Total demo ~2–3 min.
